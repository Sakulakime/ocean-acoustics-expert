## Why this source anchors the knowledge base

COA is the main local reference for underwater acoustic propagation modeling, connecting physical waveguide intuition, governing equations, numerical solution families, broadband propagation, ambient noise, and sonar-oriented signal processing.

## Scope by chapter

1. Fundamentals of ocean acoustics
2. Wave propagation theory
3. Ray methods
4. Wavenumber integration techniques
5. Normal modes
6. Parabolic equations
7. Finite differences and finite elements
8. Broadband modeling
9. Ambient noise
10. Signals in noise

## What changed in the second edition

The second edition adds or expands material on:
- waveguide invariants
- improved ray-method exposition
- aliasing and 3D scattering in wavenumber integration
- normal modes in elastic media and realistic range dependence
- updated parabolic-equation developments
- virtual source concept and FE extensions
- Doppler in waveguides
- Green's functions from noise correlation
- vector-sensor beamforming, synthetic signal stimulation, and time reversal
