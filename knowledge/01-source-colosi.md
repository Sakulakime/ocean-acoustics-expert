## What it adds to COA

COA treats the ocean as deterministic. This monograph covers how random sound-speed structure, chiefly internal waves, randomizes the acoustic field, and the theories that predict the field's statistics:

- internal-wave dynamics, the Garrett-Munk internal-wave model, observations, other sources of stochastic sound-speed structure, and Monte Carlo simulation of internal-wave fields (Ch. 3)
- the origin of phase and amplitude fluctuations, acoustic sensitivity to internal waves, and the propagation regimes: unsaturated, partially saturated and fully saturated (Ch. 4)
- four prediction theories:
  - ray theory: travel-time variance and other ray-like observables, ray chaos and the α parameter (Ch. 5)
  - weak fluctuation theory: phase and log-amplitude spectra without and with a waveguide, the Λ-Φ diagram (Ch. 6)
  - path integral theory: mean pressure, mutual coherence functions over space, time and frequency, intensity moments (Ch. 7)
  - mode transport theory: coupled-mode solutions, mean intensity, coherence and intensity variance of the modes (Ch. 8)
- compact prerequisites: rays, Fresnel zones and ray tubes, the Born and Rytov approximations, path integrals, normal modes (Ch. 2), and a list of the book's notation (PDF 21-24)

Table 1.5 (§1.5, PDF p. 62) lists, for each observable and propagation regime, the theory and chapter best suited to predict it. Read it first when the question is which theory applies.

## Retrieval guidance

- COA has no chapter on ocean fluctuations. For questions on internal waves, acoustic fluctuations, coherence, saturation or ray chaos, this book is the first one to read, through the `ocean_fluctuations` route in `routing-map.yaml`. Brekhovskikh & Lysanov Ch. 10 (`01-source-brekhovskikh-lysanov.md`) gives a shorter classical treatment.
- For the deterministic propagation physics underneath (rays, modes), read COA's notes first.
- Read page ranges only, never the whole book. Cite the section and the PDF pages read, for example `[textbook: Colosi 2016 §3.3, PDF pp. 142-151]`.
